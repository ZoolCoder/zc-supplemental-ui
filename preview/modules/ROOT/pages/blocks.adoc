== Admonitions

NOTE: A note carries background a reader may need.

TIP: A tip shortens a task.

IMPORTANT: Important text changes what the reader does next.

WARNING: A warning prevents a mistake that costs time.

CAUTION: A caution prevents a mistake that loses data.
